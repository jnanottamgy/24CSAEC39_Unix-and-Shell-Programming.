9 · UNIT V — Shell Scripts. The blind spot.

This unit is worth 8 Part-B marks + roughly 2 Part-A marks = 10 marks, or 20% of the paper — and not one of your three past papers contains a single shell-scripting question. Every prediction below is derived from the syllabus topic list and the paper's own 8-mark two-part structure, not from precedent. Treat this section as the difference between scraping 62 and comfortably clearing 80.

Your syllabus lists Unit V topics in this exact order — the examiner will almost certainly walk the same order when splitting two questions:

read → command line arguments → exit & exit status → && || → conditional execution → if → test and [ ] → case → expr → $0 → while → for → set and shift

That is 13 topics across 2 questions × 2 parts = 4 slots. The natural split falls at the case/expr boundary:
